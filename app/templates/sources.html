Data Processing Flow

1
Export Metadata Source systems provide three CSV exports.
2
Validate & Consolidate prepare_inventory.py validates and combines the source data.
3
Store The consolidated inventory is stored in SQLite.
4
Assess Risk risk_engine.py calculates Risk Score, Urgency and Recommendation.
5
Prioritize The dashboard and alerts surface the assets that require attention.

Data Handling

QuantumReady stores metadata only. It does not store private keys, passwords or customer content.
